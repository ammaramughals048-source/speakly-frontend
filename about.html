💡 About Speakly

The Story Behind Speakly

We believe language learning should be fun, free, and for everyone. That's why we built Speakly — a place where Kuku, our cheeky AI bird, guides you through the most effective language learning journey of your life.

🌍 Our Mission

To break down language barriers worldwide by making learning interactive, gamified, and genuinely enjoyable. Every person deserves access to world-class language education — for free.

What Makes Speakly Different
🤖
AI-Powered Personalization
Our AI adapts to your learning style and pace, creating lessons that are always perfectly pitched — not too easy, not too hard.
🎮
Gamified Learning
XP, streaks, levels and badges transform study time into something you actually look forward to. Learn while you play.
🐦
Meet Kuku
Kuku is your language bestie — always there to cheer you on, celebrate your wins, and keep the learning fun and light.
🌐
3+ Languages
From Spanish to Arabic, Japanese to Portuguese — Speakly covers the languages you actually want to learn, taught the way your brain wants to absorb them.
